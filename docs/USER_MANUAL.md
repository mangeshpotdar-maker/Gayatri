# KalaKriti Arts Studio — Owner User Manual

Welcome to your complete single-artisan e-commerce store! This manual explains how to manage your products, categories, orders, coupons, settings, and QR codes without touching any code.

---

## 1. Accessing the Owner Dashboard
1. Navigate to `/admin/login` on your web browser.
2. Enter your credentials:
   - **Default Email:** `admin@kalakritiarts.in`
   - **Default Password:** `admin123`
3. Click **Sign In To Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Managing Products
- Go to **Products** in the left sidebar.
- **Add Product:** Click `+ Add Product`, fill in the title, price, sale price, category, ready-to-ship stock quantity or made-to-order production days, and image URLs.
- **Change Price/Stock:** Click the Edit icon on any product, update the price or stock quantity, and click Save. Changes reflect on the storefront instantly.
- **Ready-to-Ship vs Made-to-Order:** Toggle between "Ready-to-Ship" (uses stock counter) and "Made-to-Order" (displays production lead time, e.g. "Ships in 5-7 days").

---

## 3. Managing Categories
- Go to **Categories** in the left sidebar.
- Click `+ Add Category` to create new craft categories (e.g. Terracotta Art, Handmade Jewellery).
- You can hide, reorder, or edit category images at any time. The database supports future subcategories automatically.

---

## 4. Processing Orders
- Go to **Orders** in the left sidebar.
- View customer details, Indian shipping addresses, and items ordered.
- Update order statuses along the pipeline:
  `Payment Pending` ➔ `Payment Confirmed` ➔ `Processing` ➔ `Ready to Ship` ➔ `Shipped` ➔ `Delivered`.

---

## 5. Creating Coupons & Discounts
- Go to **Coupons & Discounts** in the left sidebar.
- Click `Create Coupon` (e.g. `WELCOME10` or `FESTIVE200`).
- Choose percentage discount (e.g., 10%) or fixed amount (e.g., ₹200 OFF).
- Set minimum order values and maximum discount caps.

---

## 6. Generating QR Codes
- Go to **QR Code Center** in the left sidebar.
- Generate and download SVG vector QR codes for your store homepage or individual artwork pages.
- Print QR codes to display at art exhibitions, craft fairs, or on visiting cards.

---

## 7. Store Settings & WhatsApp Configuration
- Go to **Store Settings**.
- Update your studio address, phone, artist photo, craft philosophy, WhatsApp number, flat shipping rate, free shipping threshold, and Razorpay API keys.
