# Simple English Guide for the Store Owner

Hello! Welcome to your handmade arts and crafts online store. This guide is written in plain, simple English so you can easily run your shop without needing any computer programming skills.

---

## Part 1: Technology Stack Explained Simply

Think of your website like a physical art boutique in Jaipur:

1. **Front Store (Next.js & React):** This is the display window where customers view your paintings, candles, and Lippan art on their phones or laptops.
2. **Back-Office Cash Register & Database (SQLite Database):** This is your private record book that safely remembers your stock, product prices, customer orders, and discount coupons.
3. **Payment Collector (Razorpay & UPI):** This handles payments safely when customers scan QR codes or pay using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Credit/Debit cards.
4. **QR Code Generator:** Creates scannable QR codes that you can print on paper cards for craft exhibitions.
5. **Live Traffic Tracker:** Shows you live visitor requests, popular artworks being viewed, and website speed in real-time.

---

## Part 2: Step-by-Step Instructions for the Store Owner

### 1. How to Log In
1. Open your internet browser and type `/admin/login` at the end of your store web address.
2. Type your email: `admin@kalakritiarts.in`
3. Type your password: `admin123`
4. Click **Sign In To Dashboard**.

---

### 2. How to View Live Traffic & Visitors
1. Click **Live Traffic Data** in the left menu.
2. Watch real-time visitor counts, popular pages, and server latency update automatically every few seconds!

---

### 3. How to Add a New Artwork
1. Click **Products** in the left menu.
2. Click the brown button **+ Add Product**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Product Title:** e.g., *Traditional Rajasthani Lippan Frame*
   - **Category:** Select Canvas Paintings, Lippan Art, Scented Candles, etc.
   - **Price:** e.g., `2500`
   - **Type:** Choose **Ready-to-Ship** (enter stock count, e.g. `5`) or **Made to Order** (enter lead time, e.g. `7` days).
   - **Image Link:** Paste image internet links.
4. Click **Save Product**. It will immediately show up on your storefront display!

---

### 4. How to Change Prices or Stock
1. Click **Products** or **Inventory & Stock**.
2. Click the pencil edit icon next to any artwork.
3. Type the new price or stock number.
4. Click **Save Product**.

---

### 5. How to Create Discount Coupons (e.g. WELCOME10)
1. Click **Coupons & Discounts** in the left menu.
2. Click **Create Coupon**.
3. Enter Code: e.g., `FESTIVE20`
4. Choose Discount Type: **Percentage (%)** or **Fixed Amount (₹)**.
5. Enter Discount Value: e.g., `20` for 20% off.
6. Click **Save Coupon**. Give this code to your customers!

---

### 6. How to Process Customer Orders
1. Click **Orders** in the left menu.
2. You will see new orders made by customers.
3. Once you package and ship the item, change the dropdown status from `Payment Confirmed` to `Shipped` or `Delivered`.

---

### 7. How to Print Product QR Codes for Art Fairs
1. Click **QR Code Center** in the left menu.
2. Choose **Product Specific QR** and pick an artwork.
3. Click **Print Display Card** or **Download SVG**.
4. Place the printed QR card next to your artwork at physical exhibitions so visitors can scan and buy directly from their phones!
